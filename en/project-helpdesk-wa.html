The idea

Many questions reaching the faculty's IT support are the same ones: changing class, graduation PINs, payments, portal accounts. The answers already follow rules, so they were written down once as an FAQ and the bot serves them over WhatsApp, where students already ask.

The limits it keeps

The bot answers only from an FAQ that staff wrote and checked, introduces itself as Admin, and never claims to be a human staff member. Anything that needs a staff decision, such as thesis credit registration, always becomes a ticket answered by a person.

In use

Opened to all students on 26 August 2026, once the go-live checklist was complete. In the first week, around fifteen students used it and most tickets were closed out.

Still open

One risk is recorded as it is: a staff member's manual reply in a chat that is still an open ticket can also trigger the bot. The rule is that staff wait for the ticket to be closed before typing into that chat.

Core features

  • A numbered menu as the way in, with a free-text path when the question is already clear
  • Answers from 14 FAQ categories that staff wrote and checked
  • Hands off to IT staff as a ticket when a person is needed
  • Tickets are closed by staff through a script, not through chat
  • Runs as an agent separate from its owner's personal assistant, inside a Docker sandbox
  • Uses its model through an API key, not a personal subscription
