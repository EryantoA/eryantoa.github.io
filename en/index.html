02 — About

I build the unglamorous layer small businesses can't run without: the system that keeps going when everything else fails.

In Pekanbaru the internet can drop right in the middle of the dinner rush. A cloud cashier that refuses orders when the signal goes isn't malfunctioning — it was designed that way. So every POS I build writes the transaction to the device first and reconciles with the server when the line comes back.

My systems are currently in use at the UIR Faculty of Law and for dissertation research. The cashier, attendance and service projects I built independently — and they're ready to be adapted to your business.
