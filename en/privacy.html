01 What I collect

This site has no forms, sets no cookies, and runs no trackers or analytics. Fonts and every other file are served from the same server as the page.

Messages you send

When you contact me on WhatsApp or by email, I receive your message and the contact details you use on that service.

02 How I use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and scope the work
  • To keep a record of quotes and agreements

03 Third parties

Only one service is involved when you open this site.

Hosting

This site is served by GitHub Pages, which keeps anonymous request logs. Those logs are held by GitHub, not by me.

WhatsApp and email

When you tap a WhatsApp or email button you leave this site, and that service's own privacy policy applies.

04 Retention

Messages are kept while a conversation is live and for as long as a project record needs to exist for tax and warranty purposes. Everything else is deleted once it stops being useful.

05 Your rights

  • Ask for a copy of what I hold about you
  • Ask me to correct or delete it
  • Withdraw consent for further contact at any time

06 Children

This site is aimed at business owners and institutions. I do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 — if that happens, tell me and I will remove it.

07 Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it.

08 Contact

Questions about any of the above go to eryantoagusriadi793@gmail.com and get a reply within 1 working day.
